# Privacy policy

Draft of 26 November 2026.

This policy covers missaquarius.org and every page on it, the Letters included. It is run by the organization that will be named in the final version. **Contact:** miss.aquarius@heartbank.ceo

## What this site keeps about you

The site has no accounts, no forms and no log-in, and its pages are the same for everyone. It keeps two things: a few small items in your own browser, and the records of the visit counter described below.

## In your browser

- **Your language and theme**, if you choose them with the buttons at the foot of a page (`ma-lang`, `ma-theme`). They never leave your browser.
- **A random visit ID** (`ma-device`), made the first time a page opens. It is not your name or anything about you. It lets the visit counter tell one browser's visits from another's, so it is sent with every message to the counter.
- **A copy of the site's pages**, kept by the site's service worker so they open without a connection.

You can remove all three by clearing this site's data in your browser's settings.

## The visit counter

Every page here sends a short message to the visit counter at thonly.org/api/track when it opens, and another when you click a link.

- **When a page opens:** the page's address, the page that sent you here (as your browser reports it), and the `utm_source` tag if the link you followed carried one.
- **When you click a link:** the link's address, and whether it leads to another page of this site.
- **With every message:** the random visit ID, your browser's time zone (such as Asia/Phnom_Penh), and whether automation software is driving the browser.

The counter adds the time, your browser's user-agent string (its name and version and your operating system) and this site's name, and stores each message as a record. The record does not include your IP address. A running count of messages is kept for each visit ID, and most messages also send a notification with these details to the administrator's device. The records have no set deletion date.

## Cookies

This site's own code sets no cookies.

## Who else handles data

- **GitHub** serves this site through GitHub Pages. It handles each request, including the address it came from, under its own privacy policy.
- **Cloudflare** runs Cloudflare Web Analytics on every page: a script loaded from static.cloudflareinsights.com that measures visits, under Cloudflare's own privacy policy. We see only Cloudflare's totals for the site, never a person.
- **Google** runs the visit counter's servers (Firebase, for thonly.org). It receives each message to the counter, including the address it came from, under its own privacy policy; it stores the counter's records and delivers its notifications.
